5 **Micro power consumption working mode**  
The wireless current collection terminal achieves power saving effect through intermittent operation. The device is mostly in standby mode, and only a very small part of the circuit continues to work to maintain low power consumption. This part of the circuit will ensure the correct operation of the system time and the accurate recording of the next activation time.  
Daily power consumption calculation of the device: The daily working state is divided into standby state and working state (waking up to collect data and uploading data through GPRS/NB-IOT), corresponding to different power consumption data states.

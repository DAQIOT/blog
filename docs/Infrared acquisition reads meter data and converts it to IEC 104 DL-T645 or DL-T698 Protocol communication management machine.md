Infrared meter reading adopts infrared communication, which uses infrared technology to achieve close range communication between two points. It consists of two parts: infrared transmission and reception. The infrared meter reading device is based on the modulation and demodulation of 38kHz signals to achieve infrared and[RS-485](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=269263713&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=RS-485&zhida_source=entity)Signal conversion. The front of the infrared probe (usually with a 3M adhesive surface) is attached to the infrared communication interface of the electric meter to collect data, without the need for wiring. When using, pay attention to aligning the infrared probe with the infrared window of the electric meter, preferably at a distance of 0-3 meters, and can be separated by transparent glass.

Distributed PV "Four Supports" refers to[considerable](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=256580746&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%8F%AF%E8%A7%82&zhida_source=entity)Measurable, adjustable, and controllable. The specific meaning is as follows:
Observable: Implement distributed PV stations Data Acquisition, collect data including active, reactive, voltage, current, switch information, etc., allowing users and various levels of dispatchers Real-time to understand the operation of distributed PV stations.
Measurable: Achieve accurate prediction of distributed PV power generation, including mid-term (0h-240h), short-term (0h-72h), and ultra short term (15min-4h) power prediction data, ensuring precise and controllable operation of the station.
Adjustable: The scheduling automation main station automatically adjusts the active and reactive power of the station through control devices such as group control and scheduling systems such as AGC and AVC control systems, ensuring that the station operates according to the requirements of various levels of scheduling.
Controllable: Utilizing scheduling automation systems[Electricity collection system](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=256580746&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%94%A8%E7%94%B5%E9%87%87%E9%9B%86%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F&zhida_source=entity)Or group control and adjustment systems, etc., to achieve overall control over the operation status of the station, achieving unified management and centralized control.
The station should be able to collect Real-time grid connected information, electrical analog quantities, and electricity data. Real-time The grid connection information mainly includes the status of the grid connection point switch, grid connection point voltage, current, active and reactive power, etc., and is uploaded to the dispatching agency. The basic information provided by the station should include but not be limited to the following. Electrical analog quantities mainly include: voltage, current, active power, reactive power, power factor, and frequency at the grid connection point; Status variables: signals such as circuit breaker status, fault information, and distributed power communication status at the grid connection point. The electricity data mainly includes: active and reactive electricity energy at the gateway metering point, hourly and measured demand, event records, and other information

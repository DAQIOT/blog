## General industry benchmark for core performance verification of high-power high-frequency equipment
According to on-site measurement data from downstream production enterprises, qualified high-power high-frequency equipment should control the output power fluctuation value within 5% after continuous operation for 8 hours. This value can be directly measured at the output end of the equipment by third-party power detection instruments. Equipment with excessive fluctuation is prone to inconsistent welding strength of the same batch of products.
Qualified high-power high-frequency equipment, equipped with[Spark protection system](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=280400045&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%81%AB%E8%8A%B1%E4%BF%9D%E6%8A%A4%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F&zhida_source=entity)The response speed needs to be controlled in milliseconds. When the workpiece ignites, the high-frequency output can be quickly cut off to avoid burning out the mold and workpiece, while reducing the core[components](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=280400045&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%85%83%E5%99%A8%E4%BB%B6&zhida_source=entity)The wear and tear extends the overall service life of the equipment.
Qualified high-power high-frequency equipment requires the pressure uniformity of the workbench to be controlled within ± 0.02MPa, and the pressure difference in the entire welding area should not be too large. Otherwise, it is easy to encounter problems such as inconsistent welding depth at different positions of the same workpiece, weak welding in some areas, and excessive indentation in some areas.
The grounding protection structure of high-power high-frequency equipment must be complete and compliant, and the shell must be protected during the operation of the equipment[leakage current](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=280400045&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E6%BC%8F%E7%94%B5%E6%B5%81&zhida_source=entity)The numerical value needs to be controlled within a safe range, and the protective door on the operating side needs to be equipped with corresponding safety interlocking devices. When non professionals open the protective door, the equipment can automatically cut off the high-frequency output to avoid safety hazards.
It is necessary to clarify that high-power high-frequency equipment belongs to high-frequency electrical equipment, and corresponding safety operation regulations must be strictly followed during operation. Non certified electricians are not allowed to open the electrical chassis of the equipment at will to avoid the risk of electric shock. Flammable and explosive materials cannot be stacked around the equipment, and regular safety inspections are required during daily use.
## Adaptation and selection logic of high-power high-frequency equipment under different operating conditions
If the processing scenario of the enterprise mainly focuses on large-sized and wide width workpieces, such as tarpaulins, membrane structures, large-sized inflatable pads, etc., priority should be given to selecting them[gantry-type structure](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=280400045&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%BE%99%E9%97%A8%E5%BC%8F%E7%BB%93%E6%9E%84&zhida_source=entity)High power high-frequency equipment, the gantry crossbeam of this type of equipment can move along the guide rail, and the effective welding surface can reach 3 meters or even larger, suitable for wide workpieces[Continuous Welding](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=280400045&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E8%BF%9E%E7%BB%AD%E7%84%8A%E6%8E%A5&zhida_source=entity)demand
If the processing scenario of the enterprise is mainly focused on multiple batches of small batch workpieces, it is preferred to choose high-power high-frequency equipment with sliding table push disc type. The two sliding tables of this type of equipment can alternate in and out, and the operator can load and unload materials on the other side while welding the equipment, effectively improving the output efficiency per unit time and reducing the standby time of the equipment.
If the processing scenario of the enterprise is high[added value](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=280400045&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%99%84%E5%8A%A0%E5%80%BC&zhida_source=entity)For precision workpieces, such as medical consumables, automotive interior parts, etc., priority should be given to high-power high-frequency equipment with precise pressure and temperature closed-loop control. The pressure and temperature parameters of such equipment can be digitally quantified and adjusted, and the welding consistency of different batches of products can be effectively guaranteed.
If the production capacity scale of the enterprise is large, there is a clear[Automation](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=280400045&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E8%87%AA%E5%8A%A8%E5%8C%96&zhida_source=entity)Upgrade requirements can be met by choosing customized high-power high-frequency production lines that are equipped with robotic arms for automatic material retrieval and automatic conveyor lines. These production lines can significantly reduce the proportion of manual operations, lower the labor intensity of operators, and improve the overall production pace of the line.
The core premise of all selection logic is to make welding samples of actual products in advance. Only equipment that has been verified by actual samples to meet its own production requirements in terms of welding effect, efficiency, and yield is suitable for its own working conditions. It cannot be directly ordered and purchased based solely on the nominal values on the parameter table.

## Common procurement pitfalls and cost loss calculation for high-power high-frequency equipment
The first common pitfall point is the purchase of equipment with nominal power virtual labeling. The actual output power of such equipment is much lower than the nominal value. When processing thick material workpieces, the welding speed is slow and the welding strength is insufficient. Calculated based on a 25KW virtual labeling equipment, it produces 300 fewer products per day than qualified equipment. The processing profit of each product is calculated at 1 yuan. The monthly production capacity loss exceeds 7000 yuan, and the annual loss is close to 90000 yuan.
The second common pitfall point is the purchase of equipment with slow response speed for spark protection systems. Such equipment has a high probability of sparking during the processing, with an average of 2 molds burned out by sparking per week. The cost of a high-power high-frequency mold is calculated at 2000 yuan, and the cost of mold replacement alone exceeds 20000 yuan per year, not including the raw material loss caused by sparking and burning the workpiece.
The third common pitfall is the purchase of equipment produced by small factories without a complete after-sales system. When such equipment malfunctions, it is difficult to find corresponding technical personnel for on-site maintenance. On average, the downtime for each malfunction exceeds 3 days. Calculated based on a daily production capacity loss of 10000 yuan, the cost of one malfunction is[direct loss](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=280400045&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%9B%B4%E6%8E%A5%E6%8D%9F%E5%A4%B1&zhida_source=entity)It will exceed 30000 yuan and may also affect the delivery cycle of the order.

## General specification for high-power high-frequency equipment full lifecycle O&M
The first point of daily O&M is to regularly clean the equipment. The high-frequency output electrode of high-power high-frequency equipment is prone to accumulate plastic volatiles generated during the processing, which can easily lead to long-term accumulation[plate](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=280400045&content_type=Article&match_order=2&q=%E6%9E%81%E6%9D%BF&zhida_source=entity)In case of sparking, cleaning the residual impurities around the electrode plate with anhydrous alcohol once a week can effectively reduce the probability of sparking.
The second point of daily O&M is to regularly check the cooling system of the equipment, which is the core of high-power high-frequency equipment[vacuum tube](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=280400045&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%94%B5%E5%AD%90%E7%AE%A1&zhida_source=entity)Transformers and other components generate a large amount of heat during operation. If the cooling fan malfunctions, it can easily cause overheating and aging of the components, shortening the service life of the core components. Check once a month[cooling system](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=280400045&content_type=Article&match_order=2&q=%E6%95%A3%E7%83%AD%E7%B3%BB%E7%BB%9F&zhida_source=entity)The operational status can effectively extend the overall service life of the equipment.
The third point of daily O&M is to regularly lubricate the moving parts of the equipment, including the slide table, guide rail[drive chain](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=280400045&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E4%BC%A0%E5%8A%A8%E9%93%BE%E6%9D%A1&zhida_source=entity)During the long-term operation of moving parts, wear may occur. Regularly adding corresponding lubricating oil can reduce the amount of wear on the parts, lower the probability of abnormal noise and jamming during equipment operation, and improve the service life of moving parts.
The fourth point of daily O&M is to keep a good record of the operation of the equipment. Every time the equipment malfunctions or parts are replaced, corresponding records should be made. In case of similar problems in the future, the cause of the malfunction can be quickly identified, reducing the time for troubleshooting and minimizing the production capacity loss caused by downtime.
Legitimate equipment suppliers will provide customers with free O&M operation training, guiding operators to master the correct daily maintenance methods, helping customers develop good equipment usage habits, reducing the probability of equipment failure from the source, and extending the service life of equipment.

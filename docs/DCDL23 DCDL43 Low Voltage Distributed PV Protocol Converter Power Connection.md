### Precautions for installation and wiring
Common problems with power cord wiring:
Poor contact between the converter power cable and the power supply; The power cable of the converter should not be connected to 380V electricity; It is strictly prohibited to connect the power cord to the RS-485 signal line.
Common problems with RS-485 wiring:
Connect the RS-485 line of the converter to the RS-485 line of the electricity meter in reverse; Converter RS-485 line A and line B are short circuited;
485 bus short circuit; Individual electricity meters or PV inverter interface RS-485 line is not connected to the 485 bus; Connect the 485 buses of two or more converters together.

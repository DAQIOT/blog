### 4.1 Ultrasonic flowmeter host
The host is a blue aluminum alloy shell transmitter, equipped with an LCD display screen and 5 operation buttons (Menu button, up/down/left/right navigation button) on the front, and 5 M12 waterproof aviation connectors at the bottom for connecting probes, power supplies, and communication cables. The model of the acquisition module is DAQ-LP-GWRTU, with a white ABS waterproof shell, built-in large capacity lithium battery and 4G communication module. Module through[RS485 interface](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=283821071&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=RS485%E6%8E%A5%E5%8F%A3&zhida_source=entity)Communicate with the flowmeter host, collect flow data and upload it to the data acquisition IoT cloud platform via 4G network. ### 4.3 External clamp type ultrasonic probe
Standard configuration includes a pair of clamp type ultrasonic probes, labeled with "UP" (upstream/transmitter) and "DN" (downstream/receiver) respectively, with a blue aluminum alloy casing and a protection level of IP68. The probe is connected to the host through a dedicated signal line, and is attached to the outer wall of the pipeline with a coupling agent during installation. The flow velocity is calculated using the time difference of ultrasonic propagation in the fluid.
### 4.4 Signal lines and installation accessories

## 5 Installation Guide
### 5.1 Pre installation inspection
Please check the following before installation:
Confirm that the product model, accessories are complete, and the appearance is undamaged;
Confirm that the material, diameter, and wall thickness parameters of the tested pipeline are known;
Confirm that the fluid inside the pipeline is in a full state and is a uniform single-phase liquid;
Prepare installation tools: wrench, sandpaper, tape measure, marker pen, coupling agent, etc;
Confirm that the installation location has sufficient operating space and good 4G signal (can be tested using a mobile phone signal).
### 5.2 Selection of Installation Location
The correct selection of installation location is the key to ensuring measurement accuracy. Please follow the following principles:
choose[Straight pipe section](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=283821071&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%9B%B4%E7%AE%A1%E6%AE%B5&zhida_source=entity)The length of the upstream straight pipe section shall not be less than 10 times the pipe diameter (10D), and the downstream shall not be less than 5 times the pipe diameter (5D);
Avoid flow disturbance components such as valves, elbows, tees, pumps, and reducers, and locate them at a sufficient distance downstream;
For horizontal pipelines, the probe should be installed on the side of the pipeline (3 o'clock or 9 o'clock direction), avoiding installation at the top (prone to gas accumulation) or bottom (prone to sedimentation);
Regarding[Vertical pipeline](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=283821071&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%9E%82%E7%9B%B4%E7%AE%A1%E9%81%93&zhida_source=entity)The fluid should flow from bottom to top to ensure that the pipe is full;
The installation location should be easy to operate and maintain, avoiding high temperature, strong corrosion, and strong vibration areas;
The 4G acquisition module should be installed in a location with good signal quality, and the antenna should be placed outdoors or in a location with less metal obstruction as much as possible.
### 5.3 Probe installation method
| Installation method | Applicable Pipe Diameter | Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| V method | DN15 ~ DN300 mm | The most common method is to install two probes on the same side of the pipeline, and receive ultrasonic waves after reflection from the pipeline wall,[Shengcheng](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=283821071&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%A3%B0%E7%A8%8B&zhida_source=entity)Longer, suitable for small and medium-sized thin-walled pipes. |
| Z method | DN300 ~ DN6000 mm | Two probes are installed on opposite sides of the pipeline, and ultrasonic waves directly penetrate the fluid with strong signals, suitable for large-diameter thick walled pipes. |
| N method | DN15~DN100 mm (thick wall) | Two probes are installed on the same side, and ultrasonic waves are reflected twice by the pipe wall, which is suitable for small-diameter thick walled pipelines and improves signal quality. |
Table 5 Selection of Probe Installation Methods
### 5.4 Installation steps
Step 1: Pipeline surface treatment: Use sandpaper to polish the installation position, remove paint, rust, and dirt until a metallic luster is exposed, ensuring a smooth surface.
Step 2: Apply coupling agent: Apply a layer of coupling agent evenly on the contact surface of the probe and the installation position of the pipeline, eliminate bubbles, and ensure good transmission of ultrasonic waves.
Step 3: Locate the probe: Mark the UP (upstream) and DN (downstream) probe positions with a tape measure and marker pen according to the installation distance (probe spacing) prompted in the host menu.
Step 4: Fix the probe: Attach the probe to the pipeline at the marked position, tighten the probe with a stainless steel clamp, and ensure that the probe is tightly attached to the pipe wall without looseness.
Step 5: Connect the signal line: Connect the UP probe cable to the host UP terminal, and connect the DN probe cable to the DN terminal, paying attention to the red positive and black negative polarity.
Step 6: Signal detection: Enter the host menu to check the signal strength Q value. The Q value should be between 60-100 and stable, otherwise the probe position needs to be adjusted or the coupling agent needs to be reapplied.
Step 7: Install the host and acquisition module: Fix the host and acquisition module on the wall or bracket, connect the RS485 communication line and power supply line, and fix the 4G antenna.
Step 8: Power on Test: Power on the acquisition module (insert battery or connect power), confirm that the host display is normal, the 4G indicator light of the acquisition module flashes, and the platform device is online.

### 6.2 System Wiring Diagram
The system wiring relationship is as follows: ultrasonic probe (UP/DN) → flowmeter host (probe interface) → RS485 communication line → 4G battery powered acquisition module (RS485 interface) → 4G network → data acquisition IoT cloud platform. The acquisition module also provides working power to the host.
Wiring precautions:
The polarity of the probe cable cannot be reversed, and UP and DN are not interchangeable;
RS485 communication line A should be connected to A, B should be connected to B, and cannot be reversed;
All joints should be tightened to ensure waterproof sealing;
Signal lines should be kept away from strong electrical cables to avoid interference;
4G antennas should be placed outdoors or in areas with good signal quality and cannot be shielded.

## 9 Battery and Low Power Management
### 9.1 Battery specifications
The collection module has a built-in large capacity[Lithium thionyl chloride battery](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=283821071&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%94%82%E4%BA%9A%E7%A1%AB%E9%85%B0%E6%B0%AF%E7%94%B5%E6%B1%A0&zhida_source=entity)（ER34615，3.6V / 19Ah）， It has the characteristics of low self discharge rate, wide operating temperature range, and high energy density, making it suitable for long-term low-power applications. ### 9.2 Low power operating mode
The collection module adopts an intelligent low-power management strategy, and the workflow is as follows:
Timed wake-up: Wake up from sleep mode according to the set collection interval (default 15 minutes);
Data Acquisition: After waking up, read the flow rate, flow velocity, cumulative amount and other data of the flowmeter host through RS485;
Data upload: Upload data to the cloud platform through 4G network;
Entering sleep mode: Immediately enter low-power sleep mode after uploading is completed, waiting for the next wake-up.

### 9.4 Electricity monitoring and replacement

Open the casing of the acquisition module (loosen the 4 fixing screws);
Remove the old battery and unplug the battery plug;
Insert the new battery plug, paying attention to the positive and negative poles;
Place the new battery into the battery compartment, cover the casing, and tighten the screws;
Confirm that the device is back online and the platform's battery level display has returned to normal.
Attention: Please recycle and dispose of used batteries according to local environmental regulations and do not dispose of them at will.

## 12 Precautions
### 12.1 Safety precautions
Please ensure personal safety during installation and maintenance, and wear safety belts when working at heights;
When installing on high-temperature pipelines, be careful to prevent burns and wear protective gloves;
This device is a non explosion proof product and is strictly prohibited from being used in flammable and explosive environments;
It is strictly prohibited to plug and unplug probes and communication lines while they are live to avoid damaging the equipment;
Please pay attention to the positive and negative poles when replacing the battery, and do not short-circuit the battery.
### 12.2 Precautions for Installation and Use
It is necessary to ensure that the tested pipeline is full, and the measurement is invalid in a non full pipeline state;
Strictly ensure the length of the upstream and downstream straight pipe sections, otherwise it will affect the measurement accuracy;
The surface of the pipeline where the probe is installed must be clean, flat, and free of rust;
The coupling agent must be evenly applied to eliminate bubbles, otherwise the signal attenuation will be severe;
Horizontal pipeline probes should be installed on the side, not on the top or bottom;
4G antennas should be installed outdoors or in locations with less metal obstruction as much as possible;
After the device is powered on for the first time, please wait for 3-5 minutes for it to complete network registration and platform connection;
Do not disassemble the host and acquisition module by yourself, otherwise the warranty will be lost.

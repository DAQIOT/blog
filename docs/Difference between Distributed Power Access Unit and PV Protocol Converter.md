[Distributed power access unit](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=255387057&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%88%86%E5%B8%83%E5%BC%8F%E7%94%B5%E6%BA%90%E6%8E%A5%E5%85%A5%E5%8D%95%E5%85%83&zhida_source=entity)Distributed Energy Resource Interface (DERI) and[PV protocol converter](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=255387057&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E5%85%89%E4%BC%8F%E8%A7%84%E7%BA%A6%E8%BD%AC%E6%8D%A2%E5%99%A8&zhida_source=entity)Photovoltaic Protocol Converter is an important component of thePVpower generation system, but their functions and roles are different.
The following are the main differences between the two:
1. **Definition and Function**：
* **Distributed power access unit**This is a device used to connect distributed power sources (such asPVpower generation systems, wind power generation systems, energy storage systems, etc.) to the grid. It usually includes multiple functions, such as energy conversion[Power Quality Management](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=255387057&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%94%B5%E8%83%BD%E8%B4%A8%E9%87%8F%E7%AE%A1%E7%90%86&zhida_source=entity)To ensure the safe and efficient parallel operation of distributed power sources with the power grid, including protection and monitoring of the power grid.
* **PV protocol converter**This is a specialized conversion device mainly used to solve the problem between different devices and systems in thePVsystem[Communication Protocol](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=255387057&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E9%80%9A%E4%BF%A1%E5%8D%8F%E8%AE%AE&zhida_source=entity)Incompatibility issues. It converts one type of communication Protocol into another Protocol, allowing PV components, inverters, monitoring systems, and other devices to smoothly exchange data.
1. **main function**：
* **Distributed power access unit**The main function is to control and manage the energy flow between distributed power sources and the power grid, ensuring the stability and safety of the power grid.
* **PV protocol converter**The main function is to ensure effective communication, transmission of control signals and data between various devices in thePVsystem.
1. **Scope of application**：
* **Distributed power access unit**Widely used in various distributed power generation systems, especially those that require grid connected operation.
* **PV protocol converter**Usually used in PV power generation systems, especially when integrating equipment from different manufacturers together.
1. **Technical Requirements**：
* **Distributed power access unit**High electrical performance is required, such as precise control of voltage and frequency, as well as strong adaptability to the power grid.
* **PV protocol converter**Need to have strong Protocol processing capability, able to support multiple communication standards and Protocol.
In summary, distributed power access units focus more on the access and control of electrical energy, while the PV protocol converter focuses on the conversion of communication Protocol. Although both play important roles in thePVpower generation system, their focus is different.

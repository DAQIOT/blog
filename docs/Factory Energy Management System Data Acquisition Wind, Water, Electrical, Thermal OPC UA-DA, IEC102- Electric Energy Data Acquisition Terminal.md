Electricity collection is an important component of energy management. The system collects, analyzes, and processes energy data to achieve comprehensive monitoring and management of energy planning, energy performance, energy balance, energy prediction, energy equipment, energy quality, and other aspects. Energy management includes four major parts: wind, water, electricity, and gas, among which electricity management is a major part of energy management. Electricity management requires timely and accurate collection of various electricity data[Electricity collector](https://zhida.zhihu.com/search?content_id=256969751&content_type=Article&match_order=1&q=%E7%94%B5%E9%87%8F%E9%87%87%E9%9B%86%E5%99%A8&zhida_source=entity)To input into the monitoring platform and database of the system, there needs to be an intermediate software that can communicate between the energy management system and the electricity collector.
